### Design intent

Local bamboo carries the compression, aluminium nodes carry the tension, and
every joint can be assembled with two people and one spanner.

- Parametric node library generated from a single spreadsheet.
- Battery lockers sized for a vendor's daily phone, light and scale load.
- Rain gutters route water into the market's washing stations.
